# 🤖 AI Job Application Framework

> **The job search that runs on your machine.** 

An AI job application framework built on **Claude Code**. It helps you automate the most tedious parts of the job hunt, directly from your terminal. Fork it, customize it, and own your data.

### 🌟 Key Features
* 📊 **Evaluate Postings:** Analyze job descriptions to see if they match your skills and experience.
* 📝 **Tailor CVs:** Automatically adjust your resume to highlight what specific employers are looking for.
* ✉️ **Write Cover Letters:** Generate unique, high-quality cover letters for every application.
* 🎯 **Prep Interviews:** Get customized interview practice questions based on the target role.
